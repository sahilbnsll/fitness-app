Schedule every 6-8 weeks

i
Why deload on a cut
On a deficit, CNS fatigue accumulates faster. Without a deload, performance drops and cortisol stays elevated — killing fat loss and muscle retention simultaneously.
1
Reduce all working weights by 40-50%
2
Keep the same rep ranges — focus on form and mind-muscle, not load
3
Drop 1 set per exercise across every session
4
No HIIT this week — maximum 2 LISS sessions of 20 min
5
Eat at maintenance — add 200-300 kcal, mostly carbs
6
Sleep 8+ hours every night — this week is recovery, not punishment

Signs you need an early deload

  • Strength dropping for 2+ consecutive weeks
  • Persistent joint soreness — elbows, shoulders, or knees
  • Dreading gym sessions. Motivational crash is a physiological signal.
  • Sleep quality declining despite calorie adherence
  • Resting HR elevated 5-10 bpm above your normal baseline
✓
After the deload
Most lifters return 5-10% stronger. Best results of any block come in the 2 weeks immediately after a proper deload. It is not optional — it is the plan.
